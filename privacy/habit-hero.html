1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to HabitHero: AI Voice Coach (“the App”, “we”, “us”, or “our”), developed and published by TechFusion. We respect your privacy and are committed to transparent, privacy-first data practices.

This Privacy Policy describes how information is processed through HabitHero: AI Voice Coach, our strict data minimization architecture, and your privacy rights under applicable regulations including GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and COPPA.

Summary of Privacy Principles: All profile data, kid nicknames, and custom topics are stored locally on your device. We do not require user accounts, we do not track your location or contacts, and speech synthesis text is processed transiently in-memory without persistent server-side retention.

2. Positive Character Habit Coaching

HabitHero is an educational habit-coaching and motivational routine tool designed for parents and adult guardians to encourage healthy daily routines in children (e.g., bedtime routines, nutritious eating, positive behavior, and tidying up).

  • No Telephony or Calling: The App does not place, receive, or route telephone, cellular, or VoIP calls.
  • No Device Contact Access: The App cannot access device contacts, dial phone numbers, or send/read messages.
  • Non-Emergency Tool: The App cannot contact emergency services (e.g., 911, 112, 999) and is strictly an interactive audio habit coach.

3. Local-Only Data Storage

We do not require user accounts, emails, or cloud registration. All user configurations are saved solely on your local device using standard iOS storage (UserDefaults):

  • Kid Profiles: Custom nickname, avatar color, character persona preference, and default routine topic.
  • Custom Topics & Messages: User-authored habit topics and motivational messages.
  • Preferences: Alert delay timer, active language setting, and adult verification status.

We do not sync, upload, or transmit your saved profile data to any external server or database.

4. AI Voice Generation & Speech Processing

To provide lifelike audio for character voice messages, the App uses a Neural AI Voice backend powered by open-source models: VieNeu-TTS (Vietnamese by @pnnbao97) and Kokoro-82M (English by @hexgrad).

  • Transient In-Memory Synthesis: When audio playback is triggered or a profile is saved, the motivational script is sent over encrypted HTTPS (TLS 1.3 with cryptographic HMAC-SHA256 signing) to our TTS API server. The server processes the text transiently in-memory solely to synthesize audio bytes.
  • Local Audio Caching: Audio files are cached on your local device for instant offline playback.
  • Zero Identity Linking: The speech server does not log, store, sell, or associate message text or audio with any personal user identity or device identifier.

5. Advertising & Third-Party Services

The App displays advertisements served by Google AdMob (native in-feed cards, message-end interstitials, and optional rewarded video ads to unlock additional custom slots).

  • Data Processed by Google: AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (such as Apple IDFA/IDFV), IP address, and ad interaction metrics in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy & Terms.
  • Consent Management (UMP): In jurisdictions requiring consent (such as the EEA, UK, and Switzerland), we integrate Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to present a certified consent dialog. If you decline, only non-personalized ads are shown.

6. Device Permissions

The App requests only the absolute minimum permissions necessary to operate:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to download synthesized character audio from our voice API and load Google AdMob ads.
  • We NEVER request access to Contacts, Microphone, Camera, Location (GPS), Call Logs, or Photo Library.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

HabitHero is a parenting utility operated exclusively by adults (parents and legal guardians aged 18+). The App includes an initial adult confirmation gate upon first launch. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU). Any child nickname entered into the app remains strictly on the parent's device.

8. Data Retention & Deletion

Because your data is stored exclusively on your device, you have complete control over it:

  • You can delete or modify profiles and custom topics at any time inside the app.
  • Uninstalling the App or clearing app storage permanently deletes all profiles, cached voice files, and settings. There are no server-side copies to delete.
