Frequently asked questions

Common questions we hear when clients start a custom software project with us.

What kinds of software do you build?

We focus on three areas: web applications (SaaS, admin dashboards, landing pages, internal portals), mobile apps (native iOS with Swift, native Android with Kotlin, or cross-platform with Flutter) and AI integration (voice assistants, large language models, speech recognition). We also advise on system architecture and security hardening.

Will we own the source code?

Yes. The full source code, technical documentation, database and access to the cloud services are transferred to you when the project is finished. You are free to use, modify and commercialise it.

How long does a project take and what does it cost?

Both depend on the feature complexity. A straightforward application usually takes three to six weeks, while more complex systems can run from two to four months. After the requirements discussion we send a detailed estimate broken down by delivery milestone.

Can we sign an NDA before starting?

Yes. We are happy to sign a non-disclosure agreement before you share any concept documents or business data.

Is there support after the handover?

Every project includes a warranty period for defects. After that you can opt for a maintenance package, or request new features as your needs evolve.

Ready to start your project?

Send us a short summary of your idea, your requirements or your technical questions. The Techfusion company limited team replies within one business day.
